✏️ What Is Digitising?

Digitising is the process of creating digital GIS features to represent real-world objects using a source such as an aerial image, satellite image, map or other reference.

You identify the real-world feature, create its geometry, and record useful information about it.

🛠️ What Happens During Digitising?

Think of digitising as a sequence of decisions, not simply drawing on a map.

1

Observe the Source

Examine the aerial image, map or other reference and identify the real-world feature you need.

2

Decide How to Represent It

Decide what type of geometry is appropriate for the feature and the purpose of your map.

3

Create the Geometry

Place or trace the feature in the correct geographic location.

4

Add Attributes

Record information that describes the feature you created.

5

Check Your Work

Check the location, geometry and attribute information before considering the feature complete.

💡 What You Should Take Away

Digitising is not simply drawing on a map.

When creating a GIS feature, you make decisions about:

  • which real-world object needs to be represented;
  • how that object should be represented;
  • where its geometry should be created;
  • what information should describe it; and
  • whether the resulting feature is reliable.

You are not just making a map. You are creating geographic data.
