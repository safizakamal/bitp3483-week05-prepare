How Does the Real World Become GIS Data?

EXPLORE BEFORE YOU LEARN

Look around you.

Buildings, roads, cafés, trees, parking areas and bus stops exist in the real world.

But GIS cannot analyse them until information about those objects has been transformed into digital spatial data.

DISCOVERY QUESTION How does something in the real world become something we can see and analyse in GIS?

💡 Your Discovery

GIS data do not simply appear on a map.

Someone has to capture, create, convert, import or integrate information before GIS can use it.

  • Field observations can become GIS features.
  • Features visible in imagery can be digitised.
  • Addresses can be geocoded.
  • Coordinates and existing datasets can be imported and integrated.
⚠️ But there is something important to remember.

Every method can introduce errors.

A GPS location can be inaccurate. A feature can be digitised in the wrong place. An address can be incorrectly matched. A spreadsheet can contain incorrect coordinates.

This is why the data-quality questions from Week 4 still matter when YOU become the data creator.
